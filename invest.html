01 / The problem

The listing says one thing. The agent says another.

One property shows up on several portals, with several agents and several prices. Plenty have been sold for months. Searching is free and easy; finding out what is real costs calls, messages and wasted trips.

02 / What we check

Five questions, to a human.

  • 01
    Still availableConfirmed by the agent who holds it, with a date.
  • 02
    That is the priceIf it changed, we tell you by how much and when.
  • 03
    You can visitBefore you take a flight or cross the city.
  • 04
    Your conditionsWhat the ad leaves out: vacation rentals, pets, ground floor.
  • 05
    Who holds itThe real listing agent, not someone who reposted it.
03 / How it works

Four steps. You only do the first one.

1

You tell us what you wantConcierge · free

Talk or type, in your language, as long as you like, and pick it up later. It starts with the life you want there, not with a form. It ends in a one-page brief you approve.

2

We search the marketScout · uses credits

PropLync agencies, portals and agency sites. The same property listed in several places becomes one card, with every price shown side by side. Each result is labelled unverified until it is confirmed.

3

We talk to the agentVerification · uses credits

For each property you ask about, a person confirms the five questions with whoever holds it. What does not pass is hidden, and every result shows whether it is unverified, requested or confirmed.

4

We introduce youYour call

You pick the ones you like and we introduce you straight to the listing agent. Your name is not shared until that moment, and only with the agents you choose.

Built with agents who work in Riviera Maya.

04 / Pricing

Free to start. Credits after that.

Concierge is free, and so is your first search. After that, searches and verification requests use credits. Nothing is searched until you approve your brief.

What we do not do

  • We give no legal, tax or financial advice. On the fideicomiso and closing costs we tell you what we know and send you to a professional.
  • We do not sell the property or represent the seller. We introduce you to its agent, and that is where our work ends.
  • We do not invent facts. If something is not in the source, it stays blank and we ask the agent.
  • We do not share your name with anyone until you say so, property by property.
