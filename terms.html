These are the rules for using PropLync. Read them before you create a brief or ask for a verification.

What PropLync is

PropLync searches properties across agencies, portals and agency sites, merges duplicates into one card and, when you ask, a person confirms with the listing agent whether it is still available, that is the price, and whether it can be visited. We are an information intermediary: we introduce you to the agent and our work ends there.

What we are not

PropLync gives no investment, tax or legal advice, does not sell properties, and does not represent the seller or the buyer. Consult a licensed notary and a qualified advisor about the fideicomiso, taxes and legal matters before any transaction. The purchase money goes between you, the seller and the notary; we never touch it.

Results

Every result is labelled unverified, requested or confirmed. Anything not confirmed may be out of date or wrong. If something is not in the source, it stays blank and we ask the agent; we do not invent facts.

Credits

Talking to Concierge is free, and so is your first search. After that, searches and verification requests use credits. Prices, validity and refunds: [CONFIRM].

Acceptable use

  • Give truthful information and use your own email.
  • Do not use PropLync to send spam, bulk-copy its content or disrupt how it works.
  • If you are an agent, publish only properties that are yours or that you are authorised to show.
