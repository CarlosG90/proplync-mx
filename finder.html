01 / The problem

The listing says one thing. The agent says another.

In Mexico one property shows up on several portals, with several agents and several prices. Plenty have been sold for months. Searching is free and easy; finding out what is real costs calls, messages and wasted trips.

02 / What we check

Five questions, to a human.

  • 01
    Still availableConfirmed by the agent who holds it, with a date.
  • 02
    That is the priceIf it changed, we tell you by how much and when.
  • 03
    You can visitBefore you take a flight or cross the city.
  • 04
    Your conditionsWhat the ad leaves out: vacation rentals, pets, ground floor.
  • 05
    Who holds itThe real listing agent, not someone who reposted it.
03 / How it works

Four steps. You only do the first one.

1

You tell us what you want

A 20-minute conversation, by text or voice note, in your language. It starts with the life you want there, not with a form. It ends in a one-page brief you approve.

2

We search the whole market

PropLync agencies, portals and agency sites. The same property listed in several places becomes one card, with every price it is advertised at shown side by side.

3

We talk to each agent

A person confirms the five questions with whoever holds the property. What cannot be confirmed never reaches you.

4

We introduce you

You pick the ones you like and we introduce you straight to the listing agent. Your name is not shared until that moment, and only with the agents you choose.

04 / The fee

One flat fee. Never a percentage.

You pay us a fee, not a slice of the purchase. Nothing pushes us toward the pricier property, because we do not earn more when you spend more. We never charge the owner, and we never touch the purchase money, which goes between you, the seller and the notary.

What we do not do

  • We give no legal, tax or financial advice. On the fideicomiso and closing costs we tell you what we know and send you to a professional.
  • We do not sell the property or represent the seller. We introduce you to its agent, and that is where our work ends.
  • We do not invent facts. If something is not in the source, it stays blank and we ask the agent.
  • We do not share your name with anyone until you say so, property by property.
